Score an outdoor activity at a single location using the criteria below.

The criteria and output format are the same for every activity and location. The activity being scored, its fetched data, and the dates to cover all appear at the end of this prompt.

## Data Granularity

Each source is a `{{"fields": [...], "rows": [[...], ...]}}` object: `fields` names the columns and each entry in `rows` holds those values in the same order. Hourly sources only carry the hours from 06:00 to 22:00 local time, which is all the time windows below need.

Every column is named for the field it feeds, so `humidityPercentage` in the fetched data becomes `humidity.percentage` in the answer. The units are not in the names:

- `windSpeed`, `windGust` — km/h. `windDirection` — a cardinal point.
- `tempAir`, `tempFeelsLike`, `tempWater` — °C.
- `precipitationAmount` — mm/hr. `precipitationChance`, `humidityPercentage` — a percentage.
- `tideSwell` — metres. `uvIndex` — an index with no unit.

All data sources provide hourly or sub-daily granularity, and every field may vary across the time windows below. Tide position is interpolated from the turning times, and daylight is calculated from sunrise and sunset.

## Scoring Criteria

Score the activity using the criteria below. Condition levels: ideal (80-100), acceptable (60-79), marginal (40-59), unsuitable (<40). Scores must be integers (no decimals).

The overall score and condition for each entry is determined by the worst-case factor. For example, if wind speed is "marginal" (40-59) and all other factors are "ideal" (80-100), the overall score must fall within the "marginal" range (40-59).

### Wind thresholds (km/h)

Wind speed:

| Condition | SUP | Kayaking | Snorkelling | Cycling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ideal | <9 | <13 | <8 | <15 |
| Acceptable | 9-18 | 13-20 | 8-15 | 15-25 |
| Marginal | 18-27 | 20-27 | 15-22 | 25-35 |
| Unsuitable | >27 | >27 | >22 | >35 |

Wind gust — gusts are sudden and harder to brace for than sustained wind:

| Condition | SUP | Kayaking | Snorkelling | Cycling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ideal | <15 | <20 | <12 | <25 |
| Acceptable | 15-27 | 20-30 | 12-22 | 25-40 |
| Marginal | 27-40 | 30-40 | 22-33 | 40-50 |
| Unsuitable | >40 | >40 | >33 | >50 |

The overall wind condition is the worse of wind speed and wind gust conditions.
SUP requires calmer wind than kayaking — standing on a board has a high center of gravity, making it far more wind-sensitive than seated kayaking.

Wind direction:
- SUP: Calm ideal, wind-against-tide unsuitable.
- Kayaking: Calm ideal, wind-against-tide unsuitable.
- Snorkelling: Calm ideal (affects surface chop and visibility).
- Cycling: Headwind/crosswind degrades, tailwind helps.

### Precipitation (mm/hr)

| Condition | SUP | Kayaking | Snorkelling | Cycling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ideal | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Acceptable | <0.5 | <0.5 | <0.5 | <0.5 |
| Marginal | 0.5-2 | 0.5-2.5 | 0.5-2 | 0.5-1.5 |
| Unsuitable | >2 | >2.5 | >2 | >1.5 |

Rain beyond a drizzle degrades all activities significantly. Wet conditions reduce safety (slippery surfaces, poor visibility) and comfort across the board. Cycling is slightly more sensitive due to road spray and braking.

### Tide preferences

| Condition | SUP | Kayaking | Snorkelling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ideal | High tide, slack | High tide, slack | Incoming ~2hr before high |
| Acceptable | Incoming, moderate flow | Incoming, moderate flow | High or slack |
| Marginal | Low tide, strong flow | Low tide, strong flow | Low tide |
| Unsuitable | Peak ebb flow | Peak ebb flow | Strong currents |

Cycling: Not affected by tide, omit the `tide` object entirely.

### Water quality

| Quality | Snorkelling | SUP | Kayaking |
|---|---|---|---|
| Green | Ideal | Ideal | Ideal |
| Orange | Marginal | Acceptable | Acceptable |
| Red | Unsuitable | Unsuitable | Marginal |
| Black | Unsuitable | Unsuitable | Unsuitable |

Cycling: Not affected by water quality, always omit the `water` object entirely.

Water activities: Omit the `water` object for dates beyond the 3-day data range (the start date + next 2 days).

### Temperature (°C)

Use the best-case air temperature in the window — the warmest hour, the one closest to the ideal range. Never the average, and never the coldest hour.

| Condition | SUP | Kayaking | Snorkelling | Cycling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ideal | 20-28 | 18-26 | — | 15-22 |
| Acceptable | 15-30 | 15-30 | — | 10-28 |
| Marginal | 12-15 or 30-33 | 10-15 or 30-33 | — | 5-10 or 28-33 |
| Unsuitable | <12 or >33 | <10 or >33 | — | <5 or >33 |

Cycling prefers cooler temps (body heat from pedaling). SUP needs warmer air than kayaking — standing exposes you to wind chill and a fall means full immersion, where a seated paddler mostly stays dry.

Snorkelling: air temperature is not a limiting factor because a wetsuit is assumed. Set `temp.condition` to `acceptable` for snorkelling and never let temperature decide its overall score. Still report `temp.air`, `temp.feelsLike` and `temp.water` as measured.

### Daylight

Water activities (snorkelling, SUP, kayaking) are unsafe after sunset. Cycling visibility degrades after sunset but remains possible with lights until civil twilight end.

Calculate the percentage of usable-light hours within each time window:
- Water activities: usable light = sunrise to sunset
- Cycling: usable light = sunrise to civil twilight end

| Condition | Water activities | Cycling |
|---|---|---|
| Ideal | 100% of window in daylight | 100% of window before civil twilight end |
| Acceptable | >=75% of window in daylight | >=75% of window before civil twilight end |
| Marginal | 60-74% of window in daylight | 60-74% of window before civil twilight end |
| Unsuitable | <60% of window in daylight | <60% of window before civil twilight end |

### UV index

| Condition | All activities |
|---|---|
| Ideal | ≤5 |
| Acceptable | >5 ≤8 |
| Marginal | >8 ≤10 |
| Unsuitable | >10 |

UV affects all outdoor activities. Water activities have additional exposure from surface reflection.

### Humidity

| Condition | Cycling |
|---|---|
| Ideal | 30-60% |
| Acceptable | 60-75% |
| Marginal | 75-85% |
| Unsuitable | >85% or <20% |

High humidity impairs sweat evaporation during sustained cycling effort. Other activities: omit the `humidity` object entirely.

### Underwater visibility

Derived composite factor — not directly measured. Estimate from wind speed, swell height, precipitation, and water quality:

| Condition | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Ideal | >8m — calm winds (<8km/h), low swell (<0.5m), no rain, green water |
| Acceptable | 5-8m — light wind or small swell |
| Marginal | 2-5m — moderate wind/swell or recent rain |
| Unsuitable | <2m — high wind/swell, heavy rain, or poor water quality |

Poor visibility reduces the snorkelling experience and can mask hazards like rocks, currents, or marine life. Other activities: omit the `visibility` object entirely.

## Scoring Each Window

Produce one entry for each time window (`all-day`, `morning`, `afternoon`, `evening`) on every date listed at the end of this prompt.

Time windows:
- Morning: 6am-12pm
- Afternoon: 12pm-6pm
- Evening: 6pm-10pm
- All-day: 6am-10pm

When a time window spans multiple hours, aggregate each factor as follows (these rules apply when hourly data is available; for fields with only daily data, use the single daily value identically across all time windows):
- Wind speed/gust: use the worst-case (highest) value.
- Wind direction: use the predominant direction (most frequent across hours). If tied, use the direction at the hour with the highest wind speed.
- Precipitation: use the worst-case (highest amount, highest chance).
- Tide: use the best condition within the window — users can adjust their trip to the optimal tidal window. The overall score should also use this best tidal condition. `tide.nextHigh` = first high tide turning time within the time window in `HH:MM` format e.g. `'10:30'` (`null` if none). `tide.nextLow` = first low tide turning time within the time window in `HH:MM` format e.g. `'16:15'` (`null` if none). `tide.state` = tide state at the best condition hour within the window, must be strictly `Rising` or `Falling`. Rising when tide is moving toward high, Falling when moving toward low. `tide.percentage` = tide height at the best condition hour as a proportion between low and high tide. 0% = low tide, 100% = high tide. Must be between 0 and 100. Interpolate linearly between the nearest low and high tide turning times from the NIWA data.
- Water quality: use the worst-case.
- Temperature/feels-like: use the best-case (closest to ideal range) — users can time their activity to the most comfortable part of the window.
- Daylight: use the percentage of the window that falls within usable-light hours.
- UV index: use the worst-case (highest).
- Humidity: use the best-case (closest to ideal range) — users can time their activity to the most comfortable part of the window.
- Underwater visibility: use the worst-case (lowest estimate).

All `summary` fields must explain how conditions affect the specific activity — never use generic labels like "Unsuitable conditions". Mention the dominant factor(s) and why they matter for that activity. Examples:
- Good: "43km/h SW wind is too strong for stable stand-up paddling."
- Good: "Light 8km/h tailwind, great riding conditions."
- Bad: "Unsuitable conditions."
- Bad: "Slack, 100%"
- Bad: "28% chance of rain"
- Bad: "Water quality is green"

`tide.swell` must be a string with the wave height in meters (e.g. `'0.5m'`), extracted from `tideSwell` in the marine data.

The marine model runs out before the end of the 10-day range, so its final day is all nulls. Omit `tide.swell` for any window whose `tideSwell` values are all null, and `temp.water` for any window whose `tempWater` values are all null.

The `analysis` field is a multi-paragraph AI analysis for the detail page. Use `\n\n` to separate paragraphs. Keep it to two or three short paragraphs — cover the factors that decide the score and the best window, and stop.

`analysis` and `summary` appear on different screens, so `analysis` must stand on its own — but never open it by restating `summary` word for word. Lead on what the summary leaves out.

When a time window has no weather data at all (e.g. hours already passed), leave its entry nearly empty rather than writing "N/A" or "no-data" values — omit every factor you cannot fill.

## Output

Each key in `entries` is `date;timeRange` and nothing more. Never write the activity or the coordinates: they are known already.

**Leave out anything you have no value for.** Omit a whole factor the activity cannot use — cycling has no `tide`, `water` or `visibility`, the water activities have no `humidity` — and omit an individual field with nothing to report. Do not write `null` to fill a gap. A window whose hours have already passed can be `{{}}` with only what is known.
